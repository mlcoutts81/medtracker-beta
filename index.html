MedTracker Privacy Policy

100% On-Device • Zero Network Access

Effective Date: 27 November 2026

Short Summary: MedTracker (com.coutts.medtracker) does not request Android's INTERNET permission. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal, medical, or usage data. Everything stays strictly on your device.

1. Data Collection & Storage

All medication names, dosages, schedules, stock counts, adherence logs, and app preferences are stored exclusively in a local database on your Android device. Neither the developer nor any third party has access to your information.

2. On-Device Prescription & Label Scanning (OCR)

When you scan a medication box or prescription slip, MedTracker uses Android's native system camera/photo picker and processes text recognition locally on your device using bundled on-device Google ML Kit. MedTracker does not request direct CAMERA permissions, never uploads images, and deletes temporary scan files from local cache immediately after text extraction.

3. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display local medication reminders on your phone and paired smartwatch.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger scheduled medication reminders reliably at your chosen times.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Optional local lock to protect access to your app on-device.

4. Backups & External Links

If you export a JSON or CSV backup, the file is saved strictly to the folder you select via Android's system file picker. External links (such as your chosen pharmacy portal or creator support links) open directly in your default web browser outside of MedTracker.

5. Medical Disclaimer

MedTracker is a personal scheduling and adherence aid and is not a certified medical device. Always verify scanned details against your physical prescription label and follow the advice of your doctor or pharmacist.

6. Contact

Questions or feedback regarding MedTracker can be submitted via GitHub Issues.
